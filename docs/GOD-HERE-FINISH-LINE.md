## Working rule

New experiments must answer an unresolved decision in the chosen edit. Keep source preservation, software correctness, perceptual review and artist approval as separate states. An unavailable artifact stays unavailable; a broken control stays open until reproduced and checked; a rendered option is not an accepted shot.

The next creative decision is the matched sound-and-picture passage. Once it is selected, finish the complete film with that treatment before expanding into further reconstruction or additional editions.
