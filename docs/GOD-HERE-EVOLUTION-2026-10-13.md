## Acceptance changes that must survive future sessions

- **Reduction is reversible selection.** Source records remain unchanged. Keep triggers, negation and relevant setup/response relations in legible modes; micro fragments can be admitted separately as texture.
- **Independent density controls:** vocabulary size, event rate, simultaneity, temporal depth and intelligibility are separate controls.
- **Recurrence:** return to an authored condition through variable routes/durations, carrying bounded residue; define finite closure and prevent dead ends.
- **Uniqueness:** compare normalized compositional structure, excluding seed/session metadata, imperceptible jitter and encode noise. Atomically reserve novel scores in durable history. Initial proof requires differences in at least two meaningful dimensions.
- **Replay:** deliberately reproduce a stored realized score and recorded interactions. Score determinism and byte-identical rendering are separate predicates.
- **Finite novelty:** no infinite uniqueness claim over a finite quantized domain. History failure or domain exhaustion must not silently recycle a performance labeled new.
- **Three clocks:** immutable source time, container-local time and shared output time. Dependent matte/prop layers follow their performer. Deliberate audiovisual displacement is explicit.
- **Text scaling:** test interface text, captions and expressive text independently, including long phrases, narrow portrait, short landscape and text enlargement. Earlier responsive-layout passes do not dispose of the user's later defect report.
- **Shared recording:** speaker labels and effect buses do not establish original isolated voice stems. Room-bed changes do not remove events embedded in the dialogue mix.
- **Reconstruction:** distinguish crops, inferred color, new imagery and accepted moving coverage. Preserve stable P03/P04 cross-camera mapping; photo subjects do not automatically identify film roles.
- **Existing treatment evidence:** preserve the accepted r2 color pass, A–E room candidates, stretch stacks, thirteen FX modules and prior directed proofs. Select and repair, rather than regenerate indiscriminately.
- **No paid generation dependency:** the abandoned paid-video path stays abandoned. Discover available hardware/model terms at execution time; bound each optional AI test.
- **Artistic acceptance:** keep available, implemented, technically verified, perceptually reviewed and artistically accepted as separate evidence fields, not new task-state vocabulary.
