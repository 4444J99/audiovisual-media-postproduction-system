## Acceptance

Audio must become materially easier to hear without damaging clear words, quiet responses, voice character, room continuity, or synchronization. Compare without displaying the script on the first listening pass. Captions and speech-recognition confidence are not substitutes for restored intelligibility.

Demonstrate a perceptible performance or reaction revision achieved in post; an authored text animation beyond ordinary subtitles; a tracked face/object/movement emphasis; and a moving close-up whose source pixel budget and enhancement method are disclosed. Report recovered/source-supported vs reconstructed detail honestly.

All five project regions must be independently controllable, with neutral state, boundary isolation, and identity continuity through the relevant camera cuts. Fine masks and generated environments need not block the initial hard-region implementation. Defer an enhancement that fails motion/identity review rather than using one impressive still as evidence of success.

The final decision must distinguish technical pass, perceptual review, and artistic acceptance. The next whole-project milestone is the complete new linear edition, not a collection of unrelated effect tests.
