## Human review already made concrete

`review.html` presents matched A/B/C/D videos, randomized audio trial labels, and a downloadable listening-review record. Listen first without the screenplay; then resolve words against the recording. Use headphones and the phone speaker. Identify specific failing intervals before any phrase reconstruction.

Original media stay unchanged. Newly authored expression, masks, typography, altered dialogue order, and effect returns carry distinct provenance. Newly invented words have not been presented as recovered speech.
